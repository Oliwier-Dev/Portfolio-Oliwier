Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Summary

This website is a portfolio. I do not collect personal data for marketing or advertising. The site uses privacy-focused analytics and performance monitoring provided by Vercel, and the optional Ask Oliwier feature sends submitted questions to Groq for model processing.

What data we collect

I do not ask for names, emails, or other directly identifiable personal information through this site. Do not include sensitive or private information in a question to Ask Oliwier.

The site does not store a theme preference or use an account system.

The site also uses Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights. These tools collect anonymized technical information such as page views, browser type, device type, approximate location (country level), and performance metrics. No cookies are used for analytics purposes.

How we use data

Anonymous analytics are used to understand page usage and improve site performance.

Analytics and performance data are used to understand how the site is used and to improve performance, reliability, and user experience. This data is not sold or used for advertising.

Questions submitted to Ask Oliwier are used only to generate the requested answer. The portfolio does not intentionally save the conversation, place it in local storage, or attach it to an analytics event.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use tracking cookies or store a visual theme preference.

Analytics provided by Vercel do not rely on cookies and are designed to be privacy-focused.

You can limit analytics through your browser’s privacy controls or content-blocking settings.

Third-party services

This site uses Vercel for hosting, analytics, and performance monitoring. Data processed through these services may be handled according to Vercel’s own privacy policies.

Ask Oliwier uses Groq to process submitted questions and generate answers. Question content is sent from the portfolio’s server to Groq and is subject to Groq’s applicable privacy and data-handling terms.

The site also links to third-party profiles (for example X, GitHub, Instagram). Those sites are separate and have their own privacy policies. I am not responsible for how those sites collect or handle data.

Security

Because the site does not store personal data directly, risk exposure is minimal. Standard hosting and security practices are followed. No sensitive personal information is stored on this site.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13. No personal data is knowingly collected from minors.

Policy changes

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
